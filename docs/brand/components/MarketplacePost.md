# MarketplacePost

The second photo on every Facebook Marketplace listing. Photo one is always the real sign; this one tells people what it is, what it costs and how to order.

- 1080 × 1080, char ground. Fixed colours: it looks the same in both themes.
- Provide: the sign photo (cropped 936 × 500 into the birch panel), the product name, the starting price and the two lines below it.
- Product name in Alfa Slab One at 88px in birch; price at 56px in maple-bright; details in Archivo 500 at 30px.
- The reversed stamp sits bottom right, 200px, inside the 72px margin.
- Export at 1080px PNG. Keep every word inside the 72px margin so Marketplace's crop doesn't cut it.
