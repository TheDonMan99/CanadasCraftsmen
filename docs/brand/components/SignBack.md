# SignBack

Every sign leaves with the maker's stamp engraved on the back. It turns a Marketplace purchase into a brand people remember and look up again.

- Engrave `cc-stamp-laser.svg` at 40–50mm, then two lines of Archivo: where it was cut, and the order number and month.
- Engrave the text in LightBurn at the same power as the stamp so both burn to the same kerf brown.
- Place it top-centre on the back, clear of the keyhole slots and hanging hardware.
- Provide the order number from the order system so a customer who comes back can be matched to their sign.
