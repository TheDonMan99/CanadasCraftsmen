# BusinessCard

A 3.5 × 2 in card to hand out at pickup and tuck into every order.

- Front: char with the stacked reversed logo, centred, nothing else.
- Back: paper, name in Alfa Slab One, role and details in Archivo, the leaf top right in maple.
- Provide your name, and swap in a phone number or email if you want customers to reach you off Marketplace.
- Print on heavy uncoated stock (16 pt or more); uncoated keeps the char from looking plastic. Bleed 0.125 in, safe zone 0.125 in.
