# LogoClearSpace

How much room the logo needs and how small it can go.

- Keep clear space equal to the cap height of "CRAFTSMEN" on all four sides. Nothing (text, photo edges, other logos) enters it.
- Minimum sizes: horizontal 140px or 35mm wide, stacked 120px or 30mm, stamp 64px or 20mm, leaf alone 16px.
- On char, use the reversed files, not the light ones on a white box. The light logos in this preview sit on paper because they are drawn for it.
